The organisation

Research first. Engineering always.

TSI began from a simple observation: the models reshaping software are understood in depth by very few teams. Most organisations interact with them through APIs, with little visibility into how architecture, data and training decisions determine what a model can do.

We are taking the long route. Over the {{ brand.programme }} programme, TSI will progress from implementing transformers from first principles, through controlled architecture research and dataset engineering, to the independent pretraining of a base model.

Every stage is documented, reproducible and shared, so that the knowledge we build is durable and can be examined by others.

Long-term ambition

Independent foundation-model research.

Beyond 2029, TSI aims to operate as a research laboratory capable of designing and training foundation models independently — and of contributing findings, tools and documentation back to the wider research community. It is a long horizon, approached one verifiable step at a time.
